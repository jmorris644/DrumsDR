# Drummer feedback — July 2026 (verbatim source of truth)

Joe's drummer partner sent this. Page-1 app tweaks are DONE (see below). The
paradiddle text-campaign is the next big track (not started).

## Page-1 requests (DONE, deployed to https://drumsdr.web.app)
- See it as a **two-bar phrase**: a line of 8 with a space between the two sets
  of four figures. Cover **every combination** of the 16 figures paired with
  each other (1,1 / 1,2 / 1,3 … 2,2 / 2,1 …) — 256 two-bar phrases.
- Also a **four-bar phrase** view (much more material). Started with 2 bars.
- **Landscape horizontal scroll**: when the phone is turned sideways, figures
  scroll left→right across the screen, highlighting each as it moves.
- **Metronome in 8th notes instead of 16ths** so 40 bpm feels half as fast →
  wider slow↔fast range.

## Paradiddle text-message campaign (NOT started — business + build)
- Daily SMS: drummers sign up, get one new paradiddle combo per day until they
  finish the set, then a new set unlocks. Or they choose hands / feet / combo by
  skill level.
- Monetization idea: ~$3.99/month subscription. Ambition: 5k subscribers ≈
  $20k/month to split. Open question he asked Joe: how to integrate with the app
  — e.g. higher-priced app purchase that *includes* the text campaign, or keep
  them separate.
- This is a separate backend product (SMS provider + subscriptions/billing),
  essentially independent of the static webapp.
